Become an early tester Read the problem

Consuming feels like progress. It isn't.

When it is time to practice what we learned, most of us open something new instead. Reading, watching and saving all feel productive, without the hard part. At the end of the day, we own nothing.

  • Saved, never opened

    Dozens of saved posts sit untouched for months.

  • Hours, not skills

    Hours a day go into content that is forgotten within a week.

  • A goal without a path

    The goal is real, like a startup or a new role. The sources give no way to practice.

What we have learned so far

“I need this”
A paid course, an exam, a task at work next week. This kind of learning sticks. It does not need us.
“This is interesting”
Feeds and videos. It is entertainment, and that is fine.
The gap
The painful case sits in between: a real goal, but content with no built-in way to practice. That gap is where Maverich starts.

How Maverich will work

  1. Your goal is the unit, not the article.

    You start from what you are trying to do. Content only matters if it moves that goal.

  2. One question before the next video.

    When you reach for more content, Maverich asks: what would show you own the last one?

  3. Different knowledge, different proof.

    A concept: explain it in your own words. A skill: learn it only while doing it. A mindset: one at a time, applied before the next.

  4. AI does the busywork. You do the practice.

    Built on Claude. It links what you save to your goal, proposes the smallest next practice step, and checks your evidence.

Who it is for

For

  • Aspiring founders
  • Career switchers learning from free content
  • Engineers picking up a new field on their own

Not for
